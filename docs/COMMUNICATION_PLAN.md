# Communication Plan

**Status:** Proposed plan, 15 October 2026. This is a solo-project planning aid, not a record of communications already sent or meetings already held. Use official course instructions if they specify different channels or cadence.

| Communication | Audience | Purpose / content | Proposed channel and timing | Owner / record |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly progress and scope review | Student / primary developer | Compare the week’s intended work with completed artifacts; update priorities, risks, and evidence. | Private project review once each week. | Student; update `PROJECT_PLAN.md`, `RISKS.md`, and relevant status documents. |
| Assessment clarification | Unit teaching team | Confirm official dates, rubric interpretation, individual-project expectations, authentication scope, and required forms. | Official unit channel; send when questions arise and early enough to act on answers. | Student; retain response date and a short factual decision note. No contact is assumed to have happened. |
| Sprint checkpoint / workshop update | Teaching staff or assessor, if required | Report actual progress, evidence, blockers, and next steps. | Course-prescribed workshop or submission channel at confirmed checkpoints. | Student; record only checkpoints actually attended and content actually submitted. |
| Usability or accessibility feedback | Intended users, if recruited and permitted | Gather feedback on clarity, navigation, status labels, and accessibility. | Only through a method permitted by course and university guidance; agree scope and consent first. | Student; record method and findings only if the activity takes place. No participant feedback currently exists. |
| Hardware feasibility discussion | Teaching staff or device owner, if applicable | Establish whether compatible equipment, APIs, or a permitted test opportunity exist. | Direct course-approved channel when a concrete device or question is available. | Student; document device model and constraints only when confirmed. |
| Final demonstration and handover | Assessor / teaching team | Show the verified prototype workflow, state limitations, and point to evidence and submitted materials. | Format and date set by official assessment instructions. | Student; prepare a timed demo checklist and capture outcomes after confirmation. |

## Communication principles

1. Keep claims tied to evidence: distinguish implemented source, checks run, and workflows manually verified.
2. Identify all readings as synthetic until real hardware integration is completed and validated.
3. Do not share real health data, credentials, or personal information in project artifacts or demonstrations.
4. Raise schedule, hardware, and authentication decisions early because they can change scope materially.
5. Keep a brief dated decision record for confirmed guidance that changes the plan; do not invent meeting minutes or approvals.
